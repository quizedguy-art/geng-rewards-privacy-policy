2. Usage Access Permission (PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS) Disclosure

To provide our screen time wellness rewards system, Reel n Earn requests Android's PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS permission.

Important Notice on Usage Access:
• Purpose: This permission is used strictly to calculate your aggregate daily screen time duration (in milliseconds) to determine if you have met your daily wellness goals.
• What We Process: Only the total elapsed duration of device usage is calculated.
• What We DO NOT Collect: We NEVER collect, monitor, read, or store individual app names, browsing history, messages, keystrokes, personal content, or sensitive app data.
